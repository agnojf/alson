# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric after the acceptance criteria pass/fail check to score overall quality of the request brief.

## Score Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Unusable or missing |
| 2 | Major gaps |
| 3 | Basic but weak |
| 5 | Acceptable |
| 8 | Strong |
| 13 | Excellent / decision-ready |

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem clarity | The problem or opportunity is precise, specific, and grounded in the situation. |
| 2 | Outcome definition | The desired outcome is specific, observable, and tied to a measurable change. |
| 3 | Ownership clarity | The owner and decision authority are clearly established. |
| 4 | Brief completeness | The brief covers all relevant areas (situation, request details, people, timing, constraints, risks) without major omissions. |
| 5 | Stakeholder awareness | Affected parties beyond the requester are identified. |
| 6 | Risk visibility | Known risks, open questions, and safety gaps are documented. |
| 7 | Decision confidence | The brief provides enough information for the recipient to determine the next action. |
| 8 | Conciseness | No filler paragraphs, repetition, or unnecessary sections. Document leads with the answer. |

## Percentage Conversion

```text
Quality % = (total awarded points / total possible points) x 100
total possible points = number of criteria x 13
Passing threshold: 95%
```

A failure on any mandatory acceptance criterion blocks passing regardless of rubric score.
