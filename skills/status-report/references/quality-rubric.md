# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric after the acceptance criteria pass/fail check to score overall quality.

## Score Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Unusable or missing |
| 2 | Major gaps |
| 3 | Basic but weak |
| 5 | Acceptable |
| 8 | Strong |
| 13 | Excellent / decision-ready |

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Section completeness | All content model sections present with appropriate content |
| 2 | RAG accuracy | Every RAG assignment follows the status model rules |
| 3 | Evidence traceability | Health claims reference specific supplied or referenced evidence |
| 4 | Scan speed | A stakeholder can identify overall health and key decisions in under 10 seconds |
| 5 | Structure | Headings, tables, and labels make structure immediately visible |
| 6 | Executive utility | The header RAG, key metrics, and health dashboard provide a clear project pulse at a glance |
| 7 | Risk and variance visibility | Overdue milestones, at-risk dimensions, and negative trends are easy to spot |
| 8 | Decision actionability | Decisions have owners and due dates; actions have owners and status |
| 9 | Formatting | Valid Markdown; tables and lists render correctly |
| 10 | Conciseness | No filler paragraphs, repetition, or unnecessary sections. Document leads with the answer. |

## Percentage Conversion

```text
Quality % = (total awarded points / total possible points) x 100
total possible points = number of criteria x 13
Passing threshold: 95%
```

A critical acceptance-criteria failure blocks passing regardless of rubric score.
